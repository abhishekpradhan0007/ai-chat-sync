Privacy Policy

AI Chat Sync — Chrome extension · Last updated 24 August 2026

AI Chat Sync handles your AI-chat content only to save it as local Markdown and generate navigation files in a folder you choose. Conversation content is not sent to the developer, an advertising or analytics provider, or a publisher-operated server.

The extension stores exported files and limited operational information locally on your device, as described below.

What the extension accesses

AI Chat Sync works on these supported websites:

  • claude.ai
  • chatgpt.com and chat.openai.com
  • grok.com
  • gemini.google.com
  • www.perplexity.ai

Depending on the feature you use, the extension may handle the conversation currently open when you select Sync this conversation; conversations returned by a full-history backup that you start on ChatGPT, Claude, or Grok; and supported conversations in open tabs when you explicitly enable background sync.

The information handled may include conversation titles, messages, identifiers, timestamps, source URLs, attachments, generated files, artifacts, citations, generated-image URLs, project instructions, and project document text. Availability varies by provider and by the options you enable.

The extension uses your existing signed-in browser session to request your conversations from the same provider. It does not ask for or store your provider password. Authentication information needed for a same-provider request is used temporarily and is not included in exported files.

How the information is used

AI Chat Sync uses the information only to:

  • create or update Markdown, attachment, artifact, and project files in the folder you selected;
  • generate a local AI Memory Home, topic hubs, categories, and links between saved notes;
  • show sync status and whether a capture was full-history or visible-page only;
  • avoid rewriting unchanged conversations and prevent filename collisions;
  • maintain recent local activity and error information for troubleshooting; and
  • verify a licence locally on your device.

The information is not used for advertising, profiling, credit decisions, model training, or any purpose unrelated to the extension's user-facing chat-export and local-memory features.

Where information is stored

Your selected folder

Conversation exports and generated navigation pages are written under AI Chats/ in the folder you select. Chrome's folder picker grants the extension read/write access to the selected folder. The current version uses that access to create and update its own exported files and to remove obsolete topic-hub files generated by the extension. It does not scan unrelated files in your vault.

The extension cannot access folders outside the folder you select through the picker. You can revoke the folder permission or choose a different folder.

Local browser storage

AI Chat Sync keeps limited operational information on your device, including:

  • extension settings and the background-sync interval;
  • conversation identifiers, content hashes, file paths, titles, providers, categories, topics, timestamps, message counts, and capture scope;
  • filename ownership, generated-index hashes, and recent sync/error activity;
  • the selected-folder permission handle, stored locally by the extension; and
  • the locally entered licence key and information embedded in a valid key, which may include an email address, plan, issue time, and order identifier.

Full conversation message bodies are not copied into Chrome's local extension storage; they are written to the folder you selected.

Network requests and sharing

AI Chat Sync makes HTTPS requests only to the supported AI provider whose content is being saved, using your existing signed-in browser session. A full-history backup may request multiple conversations from that same provider even when they are not individually open.

This version does not operate a cloud server and does not transmit conversation content, vault contents, folder handles, operational metadata, or licence keys to the developer. It does not load remote code. Extension pages declare connect-src 'none'; provider requests are made only from the relevant provider's content script and are limited by the manifest's host permissions.

No user information is sold, rented, or shared with advertising networks, data brokers, analytics providers, or other third parties. The AI providers remain independent third parties and process your signed-in requests under their own terms and privacy policies.

If you separately use Obsidian Sync, cloud backup, or another folder-sync service, that service may copy files from your selected folder according to your settings and its own privacy policy. AI Chat Sync does not control those services.

Your choices and data retention

Background sync is optional. You can disable it, change its interval, disable optional artifact/file/project export, remove a stored licence, change or revoke folder access, and delete exported files from your folder.

Chrome-managed local browser storage is normally removed when the extension is uninstalled. Uninstalling AI Chat Sync does not delete files already written to your selected folder. Deleting a conversation at an AI provider also does not automatically delete the corresponding local export.

AI Chat Sync cannot directly read conversations stored only inside the ChatGPT or Claude desktop applications. A desktop-originated conversation can be included only if the provider makes it available in the same account's web history. Temporary, incognito, or desktop-local sessions that do not appear in web history are not captured by this version.

Chrome Web Store Limited Use

AI Chat Sync uses user information only as necessary to provide and maintain its disclosed chat-export and local-memory features. The use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Security

The extension limits access to the supported provider domains and the folder you explicitly select. Provider requests use HTTPS. Extension pages use a restrictive Content Security Policy and do not load remote scripts.

No software or local-storage method can be guaranteed completely secure. Avoid exporting confidential conversations unless you understand the risks of storing them as local files and of any separate synchronization service you configure.

Children

AI Chat Sync is not directed to children under 13.

Changes to this policy

Material changes will be reflected in an updated policy and a revised date above. If a future version introduces publisher-operated servers or online licence validation, this policy and the Chrome Web Store disclosures will be updated before that version is released.
