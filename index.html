Privacy Policy

AI Chat Sync — Chrome extension · Last updated 22 August 2026

AI Chat Sync does not collect, transmit, store, or sell any of your data. Everything the extension reads stays on your own computer, in a folder you choose. There is no server to send it to.

What the extension accesses

To do its job, AI Chat Sync reads conversations on AI chat sites you are already signed in to and already viewing:

  • claude.ai
  • chatgpt.com and chat.openai.com
  • grok.com
  • gemini.google.com
  • chat.deepseek.com
  • www.perplexity.ai
  • copilot.microsoft.com
  • chat.mistral.ai
  • chat.qwen.ai
  • www.meta.ai

It reads conversation text, titles and timestamps, and on Claude also artifacts, file attachments and project documents.

Where that data goes

Only to a folder on your own computer that you explicitly select. The extension uses the browser's File System Access API, which means:

  • It cannot see or touch any folder other than the one you picked.
  • You grant that access yourself, once, through Chrome's own folder picker.
  • You can revoke it at any time.

What we do not do

  • We do not operate a server. There is nowhere for your data to be sent.
  • We do not collect analytics, telemetry, crash reports or usage statistics.
  • We do not transmit your conversations to the developer or any third party.
  • We do not sell, rent or share data with anyone.
  • We do not use your data for advertising or to train any model.

Network requests

AI Chat Sync makes network requests only to the AI provider whose page you are currently viewing, using your existing browser session, in order to read your own conversations. On claude.ai it calls claude.ai's own endpoints, and so on.

No request is ever made to any other host. This is enforced technically, not merely promised: the extension's pages declare connect-src 'none' in their Content Security Policy, which instructs Chrome to block outbound connections from them at the browser level.

Permissions and why they are needed

PermissionWhy
storageRemember your settings and which conversations were already saved, so unchanged chats are not rewritten.
alarmsRun the periodic background sync at your chosen interval.
tabsIdentify which of your open tabs are AI chat pages that need syncing.
scriptingRead the conversation from the page.
offscreenHold the folder handle and write files to disk. Chrome service workers cannot do this themselves.
activeTabSync the conversation in the tab you are currently viewing.
Host access to the chat sites listed aboveRead your own conversations on those sites, and nothing else.

Data retention

The extension keeps a small local record of which conversations have been synced (an ID and a content hash) so it can skip unchanged chats. This lives in Chrome's local extension storage on your machine and is deleted when you uninstall the extension.

Children

AI Chat Sync is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

Material changes will be reflected in an updated version of the extension and in the date at the top of this page.
